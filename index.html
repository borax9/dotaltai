Cultural Exchange Program in the USA

USA Summer Camp Counselors Program

Spend 9-15 weeks at an American summer camp. Work as a counselor, earn a stipend, develop lifelong friendships, and experience an authentic cultural exchange in the United States.

Your Role as a Camp Counselor

Camp counselors are the heart of American summer camps. You guide, support, and inspire campers while enjoying an unforgettable cultural exchange experience. Duties include daily supervision, activity leadership, and ensuring health and safety.

🎨 Activity Specialists

Lead sports, arts, outdoor adventures, STEM, or waterfront activities using your specific talents and skills.

🎒 General Counselor

Supervise cabin groups, support daily routines, assist with camper inclusion, and participate in special camp events.

Earnings & Program Benefits

  • Stipend: Earn a minimum of $2,300 USD up to $3,500 USD for 9 weeks, with additional pay for extra days.
  • Board & Meals: Free room, accommodation, and meals provided for the entire duration of camp.
  • Insurance: Full accident and sickness health insurance covered throughout the program.
  • Visa & Travel: Official J-1 Visa Sponsorship (DS-2019 form) + 30-day grace period to travel across the USA.

Eligibility & Who We Recruit

Who to Recruit

  • Excellent English skills: Counselors must communicate clearly with campers and staff. Limited English can make placement difficult and may even lead to visa denial.
  • Positive attitude and flexibility: Camp life changes quickly! Great counselors stay adaptable and handle challenges calmly.
  • Maturity and teamwork: Participants must act responsibly around children and work well with others throughout long, active camp days.
  • Genuine interest in children and cultural exchange: The program isn’t about earning money, but about personal growth and cultural understanding.

Requirements for Counselors

Before an applicant completes the full application, please pre-screen to ensure they meet these basic requirements:

  • Availability: Must be able to start no later than June 12 and stay until at least August 15. A wider availability range increases their chances of placement.
  • Age: We prefer candidates between 19 and 25, but will also consider those aged 18–28.
  • Skills: The more specialized, the better! Applicants should have relevant certification or training in their activity area and/or strong teaching experience.
  • Eligibility: Applicants must be a student, teacher, bona fide youth worker, or have specialized skills related to camp activities.

Paleontology Education Program in Portugal

DinoCamp

A 1-2 week educational trip for students across Europe aged 7-18: discover real dinosaur fossils, join guided excavation days and work in a paleontology laboratory.

DinoCamp is an educational study trip that lets young people experience how paleontologists work. Participants explore Late Jurassic fossil country on the Portuguese west coast, learn how fossils are found, recorded and prepared, and understand what they tell us about ancient ecosystems and climate. The program is open to all students aged 7 to 18 and is organised with our host organisation, the Sociedade de História Natural.

Host Organisation: Sociedade de História Natural (SHN)

The Sociedade de História Natural de Torres Vedras was founded on 18 March 1998. It is a non-profit scientific organisation dedicated to promoting paleontological and geological heritage, with a focus on vertebrate fossils and educational activities in GeoPaleontology. It manages one of the largest paleontological collections in Portugal and is working towards a Paleontological Museum for public science communication.

SHN researchers have contributed to the description of new dinosaur species from the Torres Vedras and Lourinhã region, and the organisation has reported finds such as a Jurassic dinosaur nest with eggs at Santa Cruz beach and an armoured (ankylosaur-group) dinosaur about 146 million years old.

Designed for Every Age Group (7-18)

Junior Explorers (7-11)

Playful, hands-on learning: meet the dinosaurs of Portugal, practise gentle fossil cleaning, build a field notebook and go on guided discovery walks.

Young Paleontologists (12-14)

Learn how to document a fossil site, identify bones and footprints, and work with lab tools under the guidance of SHN specialists.

Research Assistants (15-18)

A deeper look at the science: site mapping with GIS and GPS, fossil preparation, collection management and an introduction to scientific research careers.

What Students Will Do

  • Guided fossil excavation: supervised dig days in Late Jurassic rocks (about 150 million years old), learning to find, record and protect fossils.
  • Laboratory experience: work in a paleontology and paleoecology laboratory, discovering how fossils are cleaned, prepared and catalogued.
  • Geoheritage field trips: GeoHeritage and GeoPark-themed tours of the Oeste region to understand landscapes, rocks and the fossils they hold.
  • GIS and mapping workshop: use maps and geographic information tools to see where fossil sites are and why protecting them matters.
  • Climate and ecosystems: use fossils to understand ancient environments and what they can teach us about climate change today.

Program Format (1-2 Weeks)

Families and schools can choose a 1-week or a 2-week stay. The 1-week option covers Week 1; the 2-week option adds Week 2.

Week 1 - Discover
  • Welcome, safety briefing and introduction to the Jurassic world of Portugal
  • Fossil identification workshop and visit to the collection
  • First guided field trip and site observation
  • Laboratory introduction: cleaning and preparing fossils
Week 2 - Investigate
  • Excavation days with recording and documentation
  • GIS and GPS mapping of fossil sites
  • Mini research project by age group
  • Final presentation and certificate of participation

Program Details

  • Duration: 1 or 2 weeks.
  • Participants: all students aged 7-18 from Europe and neighbouring countries.
  • Type: educational study trip - paid program, open to all students. Fee and dates are confirmed on request.
  • Location: Torres Vedras region, Portugal (SHN research centre Ci2Paleo, Santa Cruz).
  • Supervision: activities are led by SHN specialists and educators. Participants under 18 need written consent from a parent or guardian.

EU Volunteering Programs

Sociedade de História Natural (SHN) Partnership

Our Portuguese main partner Sociedade de História Natural holds a Quality Label for the 2021-2027 period under our consultancy as a hosting and leading organization in European Volunteering Program and Erasmus+ projects.

So far, we have hosted volunteers from Turkey, Germany, Italy, Estonia, Slovakia, the Netherlands, Spain, France, Azerbaijan, and Belgium. We are expanding our scope and keeping our doors wide open to all volunteers from Europe and neighboring countries!

Activities in Portugal

We aim to create a global awareness of climate issues to care for other ecosystems threatened by human activity and the conservation actions for natural heritages. According to this aim, we have some basic learning elements:

  • GeoHeritage and GeoPark-themed field tours for social awareness on Earth Sciences
  • Geographic Information System applications for understanding land degradation and climate change threatened by environmental and human impact
  • Laboratory works with fossils to understand paleo-ecosystems and paleo-climate to shed light on recent issues on climate and ecosystems
